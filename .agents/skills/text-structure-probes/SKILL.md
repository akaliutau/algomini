---
name: text-structure-probes
description: Investigate repeated phrases, lexical structure and adaptive contexts in bounded text, XML or JSON compression workloads.
---

Run `byte-structure`, `text-repetition`, `content-redundancy` and `entropy-models` before choosing a mechanism. Use `json-lexemes` for complete JSON or JSONL. Fragments may not parse; retain that fact instead of normalizing or repairing acceptance bytes.

Repeated words and schemas suggest dictionary, prefix or template experiments. Count literal tables, IDs, exceptions, escapes and model bytes. Preserve whitespace, numeric spellings, key order, duplicate keys and XML entity spelling. Measure actual reconstructed archives; byte entropy and occurrence counts do not establish a gain.

For natural language, compare sparse adaptive partial-match contexts with symbol exclusion. Update contexts visited during prediction and count model state that cannot be reconstructed during decoding. The reviewed `wiki-xml` seed trades CPU/memory for size; measure its applicability against a byte fallback on the new content.

Record unsuccessful representations and controlled ablations as development observations. Corpus-specific failures of dictionaries, byte-pair substitution or BWT do not prohibit those mechanisms elsewhere. Start with empty, single-byte, arbitrary binary, malformed and truncated controls. Final data is not proposal evidence; skills are reviewed research procedures, not executable plugins.
